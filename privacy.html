Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Stephen Jackson, doing business as Rockwell Film & Photo ("we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit rockwellfilmandphoto.com or engage our services. We respect your privacy and collect only what we need to serve you well.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Contact & inquiry details — your name, your partner's name, email address, phone number, wedding date, and any message you send through our contact or booking forms.
  • Booking & payment information — details needed to create your invoice and contract. Payments are handled by Stripe; we do not see or store your full card number.
  • Guest Memory Wall uploads — photos and videos that you or your guests choose to upload to an event wall.
  • Usage information — basic, non-identifying technical data (such as browser type and pages viewed) that helps the site function and improve.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to inquiries and provide quotes.
  • To prepare contracts, invoices, and process payments.
  • To deliver and support your photography and videography services.
  • To operate features like the Guest Memory Wall.
  • To communicate with you about your booking and your event.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with trusted service providers who help us run our business, including:

  • Stripe — to securely process payments.
  • Formspree — to deliver form submissions to our inbox.
  • Cloudflare — to host the website and store media.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy and security practices. We may also disclose information if required by law.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

Our website may use cookies or your browser's local storage for essential functions and conveniences — for example, remembering a guest's uploads on the Memory Wall. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect some features.

5. Guest Memory Wall

Content uploaded to a Memory Wall is visible to others viewing that event's wall. Do not upload anything you do not have the right to share. We may remove content that is reported or that we find inappropriate, and uploaders can remove their own content from their device.

6. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as needed to provide services, maintain business and tax records, and fulfill the purposes described in this policy. Booking and payment records may be retained as required by law.

7. Your Choices & Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you by contacting us. We will respond to reasonable requests consistent with applicable law. Note that some records must be retained for legal or accounting reasons.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will remove it.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, including secure third-party providers, to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent version. Please review this page periodically.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or your information? Reach us at info@rockwellfilmandphoto.com or (412) 292-5355.
